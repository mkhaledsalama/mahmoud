Contains 5 extra concept explanations included on slides 7 & 8 of the PDF (including MYCIN & Expert System limitations).

1. B) Knowledge Base

Explanation: Correct! The knowledge base is the repository that contains the domain-specific expertise, structured as facts and production rules.

2. A) Forward chaining

Explanation: Correct! Forward chaining works in a bottom-up, data-driven manner, starting from available facts and applying rules to derive new conclusions.

3. C) Diagnosing bacterial infections and recommending antibiotic treatments (MYCIN)

Explanation: Correct! MYCIN was designed to identify bacteria causing severe infections (such as bacteremia and meningitis) and recommend tailored antibiotic regimens using certainty factors.

4. C) To control the search strategy and apply rules from the knowledge base to the current data

Explanation: Correct! The inference engine controls the control mechanism (like forward or backward chaining) to match rules against facts and deduce new information.

5. C) They lack general common-sense reasoning and cannot autonomously learn or extrapolate beyond explicit rules

Explanation: Correct! Classic expert systems are brittle outside their encoded domain because they lack broad common sense and inductive machine learning capabilities.
